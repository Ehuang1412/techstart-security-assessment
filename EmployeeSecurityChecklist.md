# TechStart Inc. — Employee Security Quick Reference

### 🔒 DAILY

□ Lock your screen whenever you step away (Win+L / Ctrl+Cmd+Q)
□ Verify the sender before clicking any link or attachment
□ Use the company VPN when on public WiFi
□ Report suspicious emails immediately — don't forward, don't click
□ Keep your laptop physically with you or secured in public
### 📅 WEEKLY

□ Install any pending software/OS updates
□ Review your account activity for anything unusual
□ Empty your downloads folder of sensitive files
□ Confirm no work files are on personal devices/accounts
### 🗓️ MONTHLY

□ Update your password manager entries
□ Complete any assigned security training modules
□ Review who has access to your shared files/projects
□ Check that MFA is enabled on all work accounts
### ✅ EMPLOYEE BEST PRACTICES

Do	|Don't
---|---
Use the company password manager	|Reuse passwords across sites

Use company email for work	|Use personal email for company data
